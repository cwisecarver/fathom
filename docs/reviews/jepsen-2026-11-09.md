Elle alone judges completed operations, so the liveness block (which fails a shard that takes no ok
write after the faults heal) is the half that found bugs 10 and 11 in the 2026-10-29 gate. 8 shards
per run. The hard mixes injected `power-loss` (one node at a time, LazyFS), `kill`, `pause`,
`isolate`, `repl-cut` and `s3-cut`, up to three at once; the harness logged 17-25 `power-loss` and
22-36 `kill` operations per run, and no nemesis operation failed with `cannot kill container`.

`rpo`: with the survivor-selection gate OFF the acked `seq=999` row was not on the survivor, and
with it ON it was (the survivor logged `PROMOTED a local replica over the stored object`).

## What this does not cover

- **`:deadline-s` was `nil`.** The liveness checker's `:late` list therefore cannot flag a slow
  recovery in this configuration; the check that bites is `:never-recovered`, empty in every run.
  The slowest-recovery figures above are measurements, not a pass criterion.
- Tier 0 only: 3 x 10 min hard mix and a 2 min fault-free run. Not the weekly Tier 1 matrix, not
  the 30-minute all-faults runs that found bugs 6-9, not a bank workload, not a rebalancer-move
  nemesis, and no clock faults.
- The append workload only; the post_migrate capture (#53) is not exercised by Jepsen at all.
- One run per cell, so no estimate of run-to-run variance.
